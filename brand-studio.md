# Positions, Persona Hypotheses & Brand Studio Direction

## 1. Two Positioning Options Compared

| Dimension | Position A (Chosen): ClubThread Quote Assistant | Position B (Not Chosen): Individual Merch Drop Store |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Core Job** | Gives club officers an instant, itemized **demo quote estimate** (*blank + front/back print estimate + quantity tier + rush check*) and routes the request to a shop queue. | Lets individual members buy shirts one-by-one through a fundraising storefront. |
| **Why It Fits** | Matches the course *"local contractor quote assistant"* pattern, keeps the customer strictly B2B/club-focused (distinct from a solo consumer T-shirt shop), and lets us test real estimate math. | Delays production until a campaign closes and adds consumer checkout complexity outside our quote-assistant scope. |

## 2. Two Persona Hypotheses *(Untested Assumptions — Not Real Interviews)*

1. **Primary Audience (Chosen): "Devon, 21 — Campus Club Treasurer"**  
   * *Hypothesized need:* Needs an itemized estimate for 35–60 event shirts to attach to a student-government funding request.  
   * *Simulated statement (assumption, not an interview):* `"I don't want to wait two days for an email reply just to know if 40 two-color shirts fit our $500 club budget."`
2. **Secondary User: "Elena, 28 — Print Shop Estimator"**  
   * *Hypothesized need:* Wants structured quote requests (*garment, quantity, front colors, back colors, event date*) in a single review queue so she can spot rush orders (`< 14 days`) immediately.

## 3. Brand Studio Choices

* **Look (Visual Hierarchy):** Restrained **Modernist** layout ([V&A Modernism](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/what-was-modernism))—clean two-column split (Quote Intake Form on the left, Itemized Demo Receipt on the right), high-contrast tabular numbers, and zero pop-up wheel clutter.
* **Brand Character ([Mark & Pearson Archetypes](https://carolspearson.com/about/the-pearson-12-archetype-system-human-development-and-evolution)):** Primary **Sage** (clear, educational cost breakdown explaining why front/back ink colors and quantity change per-shirt price) + supporting **Everyperson** (straightforward, student-budget friendly).
* **Two Honest Persuasion Elements ([Cialdini Principles](https://cialdini.com/principles)):**
  1. **Honest Transparency & Demo Labeling:** Every calculated figure is explicitly labeled **`DEMO ESTIMATE — Pending Shop Confirmation`**, showing blank cost, per-color screen fee, and delivery feasibility separately with zero hidden setup fees.
  2. **Honest Reciprocity (No Email Gate for Estimates):** Clubs see their itemized estimate and printable receipt immediately without being forced to hand over an email address first (unlike gated pop-up quote tools).
