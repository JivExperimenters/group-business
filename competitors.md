# Competitor Comparison: Custom Ink vs. Bonfire vs. Printful

**Sources Checked:** November 16, 2026 (Public US desktop pages)  
**Purpose:** Compare how three real custom-merch platforms structure bulk/group quotes, minimums, ink-color pricing, size upcharges, and turnaround timelines for student club orders.

## 1. Custom Ink (`C01`)
* **Sources (Accessed Nov 16, 2026):** [Get an Instant Price Quote](https://www.customink.com/help_center/get-a-price-quote), [Custom Ink Quotes](https://www.customink.com/quotes), [Custom T-Shirts](https://www.customink.com/products/t-shirts/4), [2026 Cheap Custom T-Shirts Guide](https://www.customink.com/blog/how-to-get-cheap-custom-t-shirts/).
* **Observed Facts (`Evidence`):**
  * Custom Ink states that its all-inclusive price is primarily determined by **three factors**: (1) the product selected, (2) the total number of ink colors needed for printing, and (3) the total quantity of items.
  * Some styles have no minimum for digital printing. Its 2026 blog labels the screen-printing ranges **illustrative**, not observed quotes; they include the shirt, printing, artwork review, and shipping: **`12–24` shirts = `$10–$16/shirt`**, **`48–72` shirts = `$5–$10/shirt`**, **`100+` shirts = `$4–$8/shirt`**, and **`250+` shirts = `$3–$6/shirt`**.
  * Free standard shipping arrives in **approximately 2 weeks**, with paid rush options available and free artwork review. No group size-collection form was verified on the cited pages.
* **Design Takeaway:** Our club quote intake form should mirror the product and quantity cost drivers, collect front and back colors separately, and include a *date needed* check against the ~2-week standard turnaround.

## 2. Bonfire (`C02`)
* **Sources (Accessed Nov 16, 2026):** [Bonfire Custom T-Shirts](https://www.bonfire.com/custom/), [T-Shirt Pricing Calculator](https://www.bonfire.com/tools/t-shirt-pricing-calculator/), [Sell Shirts Online](https://www.bonfire.com/sell-online/), [Eco-Friendly Custom Shirts](https://www.bonfire.com/catalog/shirts/eco-friendly/).
* **Observed Facts (`Evidence`):**
  * **No minimum order quantities (MOQs)**; automatic bulk discounts kick in at **`25+` shirts**.
  * Explicitly advertises **no plus-size (`2XL+`) upcharge**—every size in a campaign or order has the same base price.
  * Pricing calculator factors in product style, **number of ink colors** (tips recommend `1–3` colors), and **1-sided vs. 2-sided printing**.
  * Supports either direct bulk orders or **pre-order campaigns** that can run for up to **21 days**, then print and ship. Whether that duration is too long depends on the club's deadline.
* **Design Takeaway:** Avoiding surprise `2XL+` upcharges or showing size-tier rules upfront reduces budget friction for club treasurers collecting member sizes.

## 3. Printful (`C03`)
* **Sources (Accessed Nov 16, 2026):** [Bulk Custom T-Shirts](https://www.printful.com/bulk-custom-t-shirts), [Bulk Orders](https://www.printful.com/bulk-orders), [How Much Do Custom T-Shirts Cost in Bulk? (2026 Guide)](https://www.printful.com/blog/custom-t-shirts-cost-in-bulk), [T-Shirt Printing](https://www.printful.com/t-shirt-printing).
* **Observed Facts (`Evidence`):**
  * **No minimum** for digital print-on-demand (`2–5 day` fulfillment); **bulk discounts start at `25` units** of the same base product (allowing mixed sizes, colors, and designs).
  * Example from Printful's Sept 29, 2026 guide: a Bella+Canvas 3001 at **`25` units** receives a **`13%` discount** (from `$12.25` to `~$10.66/shirt`, saving `~$39.75`); at **`100` units**, **`27%` off** (`~$8.94/shirt`); at **`500` units**, **`32%` off** (`~$8.33/shirt`). The bulk page advertises up to **`43%`** off; the guide separately advertises up to **`55%`** off on select items.
  * Provides **MerchShare**, a single shareable link where team members enter their own size and shipping address, and charges no per-color screen setup fees on digital POD.
* **Design Takeaway:** Mixing sizes/colors without per-color screen fees helps small runs (`<25` shirts), whereas traditional screen printing (`Custom Ink`) is cheaper per shirt once a club hits `48+` units with `1–2` ink colors.
